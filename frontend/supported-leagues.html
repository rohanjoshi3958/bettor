Overview

Odds come from The Odds API. Only the leagues below are included. What you see on a given day still depends on who is playing, which sportsbooks post lines, and the filters described on How it works.

Leagues

Soccer competitions use match winner odds (who wins, or a draw when offered). Basketball, football, and baseball use the player-focused bet types listed in the sections below.

NBA — player props

Lines may appear for any of these stat types (including over/under where offered):

  • Points
  • Rebounds
  • Assists
  • Three-pointers made
  • Points + rebounds + assists combined
  • Points + rebounds combined
  • Points + assists combined
  • Rebounds + assists combined

NFL — player props

  • Passing yards
  • Passing touchdowns
  • Rushing yards
  • Receiving yards
  • Receptions
  • Anytime touchdown scorer

MLB — home runs

  • To hit a home run
  • Alternate home run lines (when books offer them)

Soccer — match winner

For each soccer league in the table, picks are built from match winner prices — typically backing a team to win, or a draw when that side is listed. Player props are not used for soccer in this app.

Sportsbooks

Lines are compared across DraftKings, FanDuel, and Fanatics. A pick needs the same outcome priced at at least two of those books. Details are on How it works.
